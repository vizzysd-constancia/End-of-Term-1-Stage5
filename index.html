Listen First: Find the Main Idea

Listen to the presentation twice. Choose the main idea before you see the script.

Important: Submit your first choice before revealing the script. Your first response is the listening evidence; corrections are for feedback only.

What is the presentation mostly about?

Reveal presentation script after recording your first answer

“Years ago, Old Harbour Square was mostly an open paved space. People crossed it to reach the ferry and bought fruit at a small market. There were few trees or places to sit. Today, the square has shaded trees, benches, and a play area. The old market remains at one edge, but families also come to meet and relax. The square has changed from a place to pass through into a greener place for the community.”
